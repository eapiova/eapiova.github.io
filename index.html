Riccardo Borsetto

PhD Student in Computer Science: Type Theory, Constructive Mathematics, Formalization

I am a PhD student at the Department of Computer Science of the University of Verona, where I started in October 2024. I am a member of the Logic group led by Peter M. Schuster, advised by Margherita Zorzi and Isabella Mastroeni.

I work on formalized proof theory for modal logic, constructive and computable real analysis, nuclei for substructural logics, and categorical logic. A fuller introduction is on the research page.
