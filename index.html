Riccardo Borsetto

PhD student, University of Verona

I am a PhD student at the Department of Computer Science of the University of Verona, where I started in October 2024. I am a member of the Logic group led by Peter M. Schuster, supervised by Margherita Zorzi and Isabella Mastroeni.

I work on formalised proof theory for modal logic, constructive and computable real analysis, nuclei for substructural logics and categorical logic. A fuller introduction is on the research page.
